Frequently Asked Questions

What is OwnTone?

OwnTone (formerly forked-daapd) is a free, open-source media server that lets you stream your music collection. Learn more at owntone.github.io.

Do I need an OwnTone server?

Yes, this app requires an OwnTone server that is reachable when you sync. The server stores your music library. Once synced, playback and artwork work fully offline.

What audio formats are supported?

MP3, FLAC, AAC, WAV, M4A, and other formats supported by OwnTone.

Can I use my synced music in other apps?

Yes — synced files are normal audio files with standard M3U playlists in the folder you chose, so any music player can read them. Note that play counts, skips and ratings from other players are not tracked; only what you listen to in this app is sent back to OwnTone.

What permissions does the app need?

Storage access (to save music files to a folder you choose), internet access (to connect to your server), and permission to show notifications (for sync progress and media playback). The app does not request notification access and cannot read your notifications.
