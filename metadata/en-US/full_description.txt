Sync music playlists from your OwnTone (formerly forked-daapd) server to your Android device for offline listening.

Features:
- Select which playlists to sync
- Automatic scheduled syncs with configurable conditions
- Works with any music player that supports M3U playlists
- Smart file management - only downloads new tracks
- Browse synced music by playlist, artist, album, or track
- Track playback statistics back to your server

OwnTone Sync downloads your music and creates standard M3U playlist files that work with popular Android music players like Auxio, Vinyl Music Player, and others.
