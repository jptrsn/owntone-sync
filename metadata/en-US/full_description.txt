Play the music on your OwnTone (formerly forked-daapd) server on your phone.

OwnTone Sync downloads the playlists you choose and plays them in a built-in player: background playback, lock screen and Bluetooth controls, a queue with shuffle and repeat, and album artwork that works offline.

Features:
- Play any track in context, with a reorderable queue, shuffle and repeat
- Browse synced music by playlist, artist, album or track, with search and per-tab sorting
- Rate tracks with half-star precision; ratings sync both ways with the server
- Play and skip counts are recorded by the player itself and uploaded on the next sync
- Automatic scheduled syncs, optionally only while charging or on WiFi
- Fully offline once synced; only syncing needs your server

The app plays music synced from your OwnTone server, not other audio on the device. The synced files are standard audio files with M3U playlists, so other players can read them too — though only playback in this app is reported back to the server.
