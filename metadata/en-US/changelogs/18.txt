OwnTone Sync is now a music player.

• Play your synced music in the app — background playback, lock screen
  and Bluetooth controls, queue, shuffle and repeat
• Browse by playlist, artist, album or track, with sorting and search
• Album artwork, cached on sync so it works offline
• Rate tracks with half-star precision; ratings sync both ways
• Play and skip counts are now recorded by the player itself and sent
  back to your server, replacing the unreliable notification listener

Fixes: scheduled syncs no longer stop permanently after a failure, and a
sync that cannot reach the server is reported as failed instead of
succeeding silently.
